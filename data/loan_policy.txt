LOAN POLICY

Document ID: LP-2026-01
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: January 10, 2026

1. HOME LOAN ELIGIBILITY

Applicants must be at least 21 years old.

Salaried applicants must have a minimum monthly income of INR 35,000.

Applicants must provide:
- Identity proof
- Address proof
- Income proof
- Recent bank statements

Loan approval depends on credit assessment, document verification, income stability, and internal risk checks.

2. HOME LOAN LIMITS

Minimum home loan amount: INR 5,00,000.

Maximum standard home loan amount: INR 75,00,000.

Maximum repayment tenure: 25 years.

The final approved amount depends on the applicant's income, credit profile, repayment capacity, and property assessment.

3. PERSONAL LOAN ELIGIBILITY

Applicants must be at least 21 years old.

Minimum monthly income requirement: INR 25,000.

Applicants must have a regular source of income.

Maximum standard personal loan amount: INR 15,00,000.

Maximum repayment tenure: 7 years.

4. LOAN APPROVAL CONDITIONS

Loan approval is subject to:
- Credit assessment
- Identity verification
- Income verification
- Document verification
- Internal risk checks

Submitting a complete application does not guarantee loan approval.

The lender may request additional documents when required.
