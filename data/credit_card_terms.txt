CREDIT CARD TERMS

Document ID: CCT-2026-01
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: January 10, 2026

1. ANNUAL FEE

Standard Credit Card Annual Fee: INR 999 plus applicable taxes.

The annual fee may be waived when the customer meets the applicable annual spending threshold.

2. INTEREST RATE

Retail purchase interest rate: 3.25% per month.

Interest may apply when the outstanding balance is not paid according to the applicable payment terms.

The applicable rate is subject to the cardholder agreement.

3. LATE PAYMENT

Late payment charges may apply when the minimum amount due is not paid by the payment due date.

The applicable late-payment charge depends on the outstanding amount and the current cardholder terms.

4. CASH WITHDRAWAL

Cash withdrawal transactions may attract:
- Cash advance fee
- Applicable interest
- Other charges specified in the cardholder agreement

Cash withdrawal limits are determined by the card's assigned credit limit and internal policy.

5. CREDIT CARD ELIGIBILITY

Credit card approval may depend on:
- Applicant age
- Income
- Credit history
- Identity verification
- Internal risk assessment

Submitting an application does not guarantee approval.
