1. CUSTOMER IDENTIFICATION

Customers must provide an accepted identity document during account onboarding.

Customers must provide valid address information.

PAN or an accepted tax identification document is required where applicable.

Submitted documents must be valid, readable, and consistent with the information provided in the application.

2. ADDRESS VERIFICATION

Customers may be required to provide an address document when the registered address cannot be verified through available records.

Additional verification may be required when the information provided by the customer is inconsistent.

3. RE-KYC

Customers may be required to complete periodic KYC updates according to applicable policy and regulatory requirements.

Customers must update their information when relevant identification or address details change.

Customers may be required to provide updated documents during a KYC review.

4. KYC VERIFICATION

KYC verification may include:
- Identity verification
- Address verification
- PAN verification
- Document validation
- Customer information matching

Failure to complete required verification may result in restrictions until the verification process is completed.
